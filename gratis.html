Case study / Gratis

Bringing local business owners into a new store

A store two months old that its neighbours had never heard of, and a walk down the street to change that.

Role
Sales Consultant
Where
Mersin
When
Jul–Sep 2026
  • Field outreach
  • Consultative selling
  • KPIs
  • CRM

The situation

The store had opened about two months earlier, and many nearby businesses did not know it existed. I decided to visit the owners in person and adapt what I said to each one.

How the conversations went

I walked into around 20 nearby businesses, introduced myself and asked which products they already used. When they answered, I asked whether they knew the store was running a campaign on exactly those products. Many were surprised, since they had not heard of the store at all, and that opened a real conversation about what it could offer them.

Result

11 of 20owners visited the store and completed a satisfaction survey
5.25items per transaction, the store target I met consistently
~20new customers registered in the CRM

On the floor

I closed baskets of around 6 items worth ~TRY 1,000 on average, followed up on missing marketing consents to keep the customer database reachable, and initiated a sunscreen sponsorship with a regional camping business. I was also part of the team during weeks when the store reached TRY 1M in revenue.

The conversation worked because it started with what the owner already bought. The campaign only mattered once they saw it in their own routine.
