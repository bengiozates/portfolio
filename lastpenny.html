Case study / Last Penny × Efes

A staff-led product series for a brand partnership

A restaurant and live-event venue, a beer brand, and staff who introduced the products in their own words.

Role
Marketing Intern & Junior Media Manager
Where
Ankara
When
Aug 2022 – Aug 2023
  • Brand partnership
  • Campaign
  • Customer feedback

The idea

Last Penny, a restaurant and live-event venue in Ankara, partnered with Efes on its Yenilik Atölyesi (innovation workshop) beers. I came up with an Instagram series in which staff introduced the products one by one, and I presented one of the videos myself.

Around the series

I helped promote live events, managed artist and guest interactions, and gathered customer feedback in person to guide campaign decisions.
