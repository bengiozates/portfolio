Case study / Pangea Art Days

Finding sponsors, artists and 160 participants from scratch

A free four-day arts festival for high school students, funded by sponsors I had to find myself.

Role
Founder & Event Lead
Where
Istanbul
When
Mar–Jun 2019
  • Sponsorship
  • Outreach
  • Partnerships
  • Event operations

The goal

Expensive supplies and courses kept many students away from art. Pangea gave them four days of free workshops in painting, sculpture, cartoon, illustration, mural and marbling, led by professionals and closing with a public exhibition. Keeping it free meant we had to fund materials, artist fees and food ourselves.

The sponsor funnel

I contacted 50+ organizations and changed the approach each time a channel stopped working.

  1. Museums and private art institutionsWe started here because public bodies need long approval processes before releasing funds.
  2. Philanthropic business ownersMost attempts failed. Their contact channels were private, and assistants rarely passed our message on.
  3. Cultural institutions, in personFace-to-face meetings with organizations such as İKSV moved the conversations forward.
  4. Kültür A.Ş. and Indomie said yesKültür A.Ş., the Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality’s culture company, became our main sponsor after seeing what we were building.

The Indomie pitch

Indomie had just entered the Turkish market. We lived in a boarding school and ate instant noodles ourselves, so I reached them through customer service with a simple case. They would get visibility with a young audience, and our participants would get practical, easy food. A local bakery and Bebeto, a Turkish candy brand, joined as food sponsors through personal networking and face-to-face meetings.

Why we got rejected

Three reasons came up again and again. We used the wrong contact channel, the budget was too large for the company, or the project did not match what the company cared about.

Artists and participants

I reached most artists through social media. Many were already booked, and others did not want to commit four days at low or no pay, even when they liked the project. Those who joined included an internationally recognized emerging photographer and an exhibiting artist and author. For participants, we visited nearly every school in Istanbul with posters, our principal wrote to principals across Turkey, and anyone travelling to another city promoted the festival there.

Result

160participants across 6 workshops
TRY 50Kcash sponsorship (~€8K), plus food sponsors
40+people on the organizing team

The yes answers came when the pitch matched what the sponsor already wanted, and when we could talk to a person directly.
